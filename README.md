Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Best Meal You Ever Had"](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85): one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best meal the entrant ever had. An AI judge reads the sentences two at a time and keeps the one that makes the meal feel most concrete, most personal, and most worth remembering.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes a single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. The sentence names a specific dish, a place, a texture, and one moment from a real memory, and it validates itself before writing: it must be exactly one sentence of at most 200 characters.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts /output/entry.txt
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later. No dependencies, no network access.
